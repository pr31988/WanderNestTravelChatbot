Churning is the practice of making repeated bookings for the same sector (or overlapping sectors) for the same passenger — either within one PNR or across multiple PNRs — by an agent, on the same GDS or across different GDSs. Air India allows up to 4 churns in Economy and up to 10 in Premium; after that an ADM of US$10 is charged for each additional churn per passenger per segment. Parameters used to identify churning include same origin, destination, passenger name and IATA code.
