Because a 14-hour nonstop segment between the U.S. and a foreign point is over 12 hours, DOT rules treat it as a long‑haul international flight. The airline must refund the checked‑bag fee if it fails to deliver the bag within 30 hours of your flight arriving at the gate — provided you have filed a Mishandled Baggage Report. 

(If the airline delivers the bag within 30 hours it does not have to refund the fee; airlines may also have more generous policies, so you can check with the carrier or I can help you look up their baggage‑claim/claim‑filing process.)
