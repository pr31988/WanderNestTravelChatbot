General guidance:
- Whether you need a visa depends on your nationality, the purpose of travel (tourism, business, study, work), and how long you plan to stay. Nationals of many countries (including Indian passport holders) generally do need a visa to enter the UK for tourism or business.
- If you are transiting the UK, different rules apply for direct airside transit versus passing immigration—check the UK transit rules for your situation.
- Always check the official UK government visa & immigration site (gov.uk) or contact the nearest UK embassy/consulate for the definitive answer and the correct visa type and application process.
Practical steps:
1. Confirm which passport you will travel on.
2. Visit gov.uk/search/visas‑immigration (or contact the UK consulate) to see whether you need a visa and how to apply.
3. Allow enough time for the visa application before travel.
4. At check-in you will need to carry your passport (and for direct bookings the airline may request additional verification documents such as a copy of your payment card or ID—see your carrier’s requirements).
