You should notify your credit card issuer as soon as possible. Under the Fair Credit Billing Act you generally must have the card issuer receive your written dispute no later than 60 days after the date you received the first monthly statement that showed the charge for the transportation.

When you write, send your dispute to the address on your monthly statement for disputing charges (this can be different from the payment address) and include:
- your account number;
- a photocopy of the ticket, itinerary, or receipt;
- if you used part of the transportation, identify which segments were used and which were unused;
- a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy, you did not receive the service you charged to your card, and you are requesting a credit pursuant to the Fair Credit Billing Act.

If you need more help or your situation is unusual (for example, the airline has already entered bankruptcy court), tell me more and I can provide further guidance on other options (filing a proof of claim in the bankruptcy, travel insurance claims, or contacting the card issuer’s dispute department).
