Air India’s waiver says a domestic-only flight (not part of an international itinerary) that is rescheduled by more than 1 hour qualifies for the schedule-change waiver.

If you want a full refund under that waiver: the cancellation charge is waived and the agent should add the OSI waiver remark in the PNR in this format:
OSI: AI CANCEL / AI ### / DDMMYY
(e.g. AI CANCEL / AI 130 / 01NOV21), ensure the entire booking is cancelled, and the agent can then process the refund on the GDS.

If your itinerary is connected with an international segment or the change is different (e.g., international reschedules), other thresholds and rules apply (see the >3‑hour rule for many international changes).
