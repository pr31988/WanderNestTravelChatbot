According to the provided U.S. Department of Transportation information, the countries that require disinsection of all in‑bound flights by aerosolized spray while passengers are on board are:

- Ecuador (only for Galápagos and inter‑island flights)  
- Grenada  
- Guyana  
- India  
- Kiribati  
- Madagascar  
- Panama  
- Seychelles  
- Tanzania  
- Timor‑Leste  
- Trinidad and Tobago  
- Uruguay  
- Zimbabwe

Note: Policies can change. Travelers should confirm current requirements with their airline, travel agent, or the destination’s consulate/immigration authority before travel. Some countries also allow alternative methods (e.g., residual treatment) and the World Health Organization/ICAO describe both approaches; individuals sensitive to sprays may experience transient discomfort.
