Under the DOT final rule, a “significant change” to an international itinerary’s arrival time is when the passenger is scheduled to arrive at the destination airport six hours or more later than the original scheduled arrival time. 

Note: some airlines use shorter thresholds (and may continue to do so), but the DOT’s unified standard for international arrival-time changes is six hours.
