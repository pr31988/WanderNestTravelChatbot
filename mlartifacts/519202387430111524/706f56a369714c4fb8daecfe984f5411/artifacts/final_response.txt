Air India’s waiver says a domestic-only flight rescheduled by more than 1 hour qualifies for the waiver (i.e., cancellation charge waived and passenger eligible for full refund). This applies to domestic travel not connected to an international itinerary.

If seeking the refund, the carrier’s instructions include adding the coupon remark/OSI like:
OSI: AI CANCEL / AI ### / DDMMYY
and ensuring the entire booking is cancelled so the agent can process the refund on the GDS.
