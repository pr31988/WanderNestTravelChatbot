Under the DOT rule, a change that causes an international itinerary to arrive at the destination airport six hours or more later than the original scheduled arrival time is considered a "significant change."  

Note: airlines may choose to use a shorter timeframe for what they call a significant change, but they cannot set a threshold longer than the DOT’s six-hour standard.
