Churning, per Air India’s GDS misuse policy, is the repeated creation of bookings for the same sector (or overlapping sectors) for the same passenger — either within one PNR or across multiple PNRs — on the same GDS or across different GDSs by an agent. 

Key points:
- Allowed thresholds: up to 4 churns for Economy bookings; up to 10 for Premium bookings.  
- Penalty: after the threshold, an ADM of US$10 is charged for each additional churn per passenger per segment.  
- How it’s judged: factors include same origin, destination, passenger name and IATA code.
