The information in our knowledge base does not list specific, mandatory characteristics (for example: expiration length, transferability, whether change fees apply, or exact monetary value) that a travel voucher must include. What the rule does say is about when airlines must offer travel credits or vouchers and what documentation they may require:

- Airlines must provide travel credits/vouchers to passengers whose travel is affected by a serious communicable disease when one of these applies:
  1. A governmental entity restricts or prohibits the passenger from traveling (e.g., stay‑at‑home order, entry restriction, border closure).
  2. A passenger is advised by a licensed treating medical professional or determines consistent with public health guidance (CDC, comparable agencies, or WHO) not to travel during a public health emergency to protect themselves.
  3. A passenger is advised by a licensed treating medical professional or determines consistent with public health guidance not to travel because they have or may have contracted a serious communicable disease and their travel would pose a direct threat to others.

- Airlines are allowed to require current medical documentation or government orders as evidence to qualify for a travel credit/voucher, except where an HHS directive says requiring documentation is not in the public interest.

- Carriers may rely on partners (for example, a marketing codeshare partner) to process and issue vouchers for ticket‑agent transacted itineraries, and ticket agents are encouraged to help by supplying information (passenger contact details, price paid, etc.).

Because the KB doesn’t specify the voucher’s mandatory terms, you should contact the airline (or the ticket agent who sold the ticket) and confirm the voucher’s exact characteristics. Useful questions to ask the airline:

- What monetary value will the voucher cover (full fare, taxes and fees)?
- What is the voucher’s expiration date and are there blackout dates?
- Is the voucher transferable or reassignable to another passenger?
- Are there any change or re‑booking fees when using the voucher?
- Can the voucher be used toward a different route or only the original itinerary?
- What documentation do you need to submit (medical note, government order) and how/when do you submit it?
- Will the airline accept documentation provided by a ticket agent or other partner?
- Can I get the voucher terms in writing?
