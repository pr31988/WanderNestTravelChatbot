Short answer: Generally no — you must not change the name field on an Air India PNR through your GDS or the airline website once the PNR has been created. 

What is allowed (exceptions and process)
- For minor misspellings Air India may permit corrections on a case‑by‑case basis, but you must contact Air India Reservation offices (do not modify the PNR yourself).  
- Corrections are limited to a maximum of three (3) characters and require supporting documents.  
- Name corrections must be completed at least 72 hours before departure.  
- If the ticket has already been issued, the airline’s required process is: create a new PNR in the correct name, issue a new ticket, cross‑reference both PNRs in endorsements to avoid ADMs, and follow airline helpdesk procedures if the original RBD is not available. The incorrect ticket may need to be refunded.

Fees and regional notes
- A nominal name‑change fee applies for POS in UK, Europe and Israel (UK £50, Europe €50, Israel $50) — check latest airline guidance for exact charges.

What you should do now
- Do not attempt to change or cancel/replace the name via GDS.  
- Contact Air India Reservations immediately with the passenger’s ID/passport info and supporting documents.  
- Be prepared for possible re‑issue, fare/RBD differences and applicable fees.
