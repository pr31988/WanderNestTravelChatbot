Short answer: 30 hours.

Explanation:
- Because the international non‑stop segment between the U.S. and the foreign point is over 12 hours (14 hours), DOT rules allow carriers up to 30 hours to recover a delayed checked bag before they must refund the baggage fee.
- The clock starts when your flight arrives at the gate, and you must file a Mishandled Baggage Report with the airline for the refund requirement to apply.

Note: If the U.S.–foreign segment had been 12 hours or less, the shorter 15‑hour threshold would apply. Keep all documentation and follow up with the airline; if you want, we can help you with next steps.
