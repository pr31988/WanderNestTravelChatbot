Because a 14‑hour nonstop segment is longer than 12 hours, the DOT’s long‑haul standard applies: the airline must refund the checked‑bag fee if it fails to deliver your bag within 30 hours of your flight arriving at the gate, provided you have filed a Mishandled Baggage Report. 

Practical tips: file the mishandled‑baggage report with the airline as soon as possible, keep copies of all documents/receipts, and follow up with the carrier — they may still try to return the bag (including via partner flights or couriers) before issuing a refund.
