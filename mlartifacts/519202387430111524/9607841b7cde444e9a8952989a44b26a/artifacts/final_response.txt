Based on the information in our knowledge base (sourced from the U.S. Department of Transportation), the following countries require disinsection of all inbound flights using an aerosolized spray while passengers are on board:

- Ecuador (only for flights to/from the Galápagos and inter-island flights)  
- Grenada  
- Guyana  
- India  
- Kiribati  
- Madagascar  
- Panama  
- Seychelles  
- Tanzania  
- Timor-Leste  
- Trinidad and Tobago  
- Uruguay  
- Zimbabwe

Note: policies can change. If you’re planning travel, please confirm current requirements with your airline or travel agent (or the destination’s official aviation/health authority) before you fly.
