Because the U.S.–foreign segment is longer than 12 hours (14 hours), the DOT’s extended threshold applies: the airline must refund the checked-bag fee if it has not delivered the bag within 30 hours of the flight arriving at the gate and you have filed a Mishandled Baggage Report.

A few practical notes:
- The clock starts when the flight arrives at the gate.  
- File a Mishandled Baggage Report with the airline as soon as possible and keep any documentation/receipts.  
- Airlines may use partner flights, cargo, or couriers to return your bag — they are not required to put it on the next available passenger flight.  
- If the airline does not refund the fee after 30 hours, contact the airline first and, if needed, you can notify DOT for further dispute guidance.
