Short answer: No — you cannot edit the name field on an existing PNR through GDS or the airline website. Air India does not permit name modifications in a PNR once it is created by an agent.

What you should know and do:
- Do not modify or cancel-and-replace names in the PNR using GDS/website.  
- For spelling errors or minor corrections contact Air India Reservation offices — they may consider corrections on a case‑by‑case basis. Corrections are limited (maximum of three characters changed) and supporting documents will be required.  
- Any name corrections must be made at least 72 hours before departure.  
- If the ticket has already been issued, the required process is to create a new PNR with the correct name, reissue the ticket (matching the original itinerary), and follow the airline’s reissue and endorsement instructions (you may need to accept a different RBD and request helpdesk assistance). Cross‑reference the old and new PNRs to avoid ADMs.  
- Fees may apply depending on point of sale (e.g., UK £50, Europe €50, Israel $50) — check applicable fare rules and POS fee details.
