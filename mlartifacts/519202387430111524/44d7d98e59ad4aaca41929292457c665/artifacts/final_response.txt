The information you provided does not specify any mandatory, standardized characteristics (for example: expiration, transferability, value or refundability) that a travel voucher must have when issued because a passenger cannot fly due to a serious communicable disease.

What the knowledge base does say
- Airlines are permitted to issue travel credits or vouchers to passengers who:
  - are restricted or prohibited from traveling by a governmental entity because of a serious communicable disease (e.g., quarantine, entry restriction, border closure); or
  - are advised by a licensed treating medical professional (or determine consistent with CDC/WHO/public‑health guidance) not to travel because they have or may have contracted a serious communicable disease.
- Airlines may require documentation (for example, current medical documentation or a government quarantine/order) to support a request for travel credits or vouchers, except where an HHS directive says requiring documentation would not be in the public interest.
- For ticket‑agent–booked itineraries, carriers may rely on partners or agents to process and issue vouchers, and agents are encouraged to help by providing contact info and purchase price to the airline.

What you should do / ask the airline (practical guidance)
Because voucher terms are set by the airline (or the carrier’s agent/partner) and are not defined in your knowledge base, contact your airline (or the travel agent who sold the ticket) and confirm these specifics before accepting a voucher:
- Voucher value: does it equal the full unused fare, taxes, and fees? Or only the base fare?
- Expiration: when does the voucher expire and are there extension options?
- Redemption rules: how and on which flights/markets can it be used (same carrier, codeshares, partners)?
- Transferability: can you transfer or sell the voucher, or must it be used only by the original passenger?
- Change or rebooking fees and blackout dates or fare class restrictions.
- Refund options: if your plans change later, is a refund possible?
- Documentation required: what exact proof do they need (doctor’s note, lab test, official government quarantine/order), how to submit it, and any deadlines for submission.
- Who will issue and hold the voucher for agent‑booked itineraries (airline or partner) and contact information.

Steps to take now
1. Gather supporting documentation (licensed treating medical professional note; government quarantine or travel restriction documentation, if applicable).
2. Contact the airline or your ticket agent as soon as possible and request the voucher terms in writing.
3. Keep copies of all documentation, correspondence, and the voucher terms.
