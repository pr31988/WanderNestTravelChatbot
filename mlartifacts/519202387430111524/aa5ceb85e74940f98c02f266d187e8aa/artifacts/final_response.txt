Under DOT’s final rule, an international flight’s arrival time is considered a “significant change” if the passenger is scheduled to arrive at the destination airport six hours or more later than the original scheduled arrival time.  

Note: some airlines use shorter thresholds (i.e., more generous policies) and are allowed to keep those, but they cannot set a threshold longer than the DOT standard. If you want, I can check a specific airline’s policy or help with a particular booking.
