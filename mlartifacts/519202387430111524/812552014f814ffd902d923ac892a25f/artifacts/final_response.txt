Under the DOT final rule, a carrier must issue a refund for a ticket paid with a credit card within 7 business days. That 7‑business‑day clock starts when the refund is due—i.e., when the airline accepts the return/forgives the debt or, for ticket agents, when the agent receives confirmation from the airline that the passenger is eligible. (For non‑credit‑card payments the deadline is 20 calendar days.)
