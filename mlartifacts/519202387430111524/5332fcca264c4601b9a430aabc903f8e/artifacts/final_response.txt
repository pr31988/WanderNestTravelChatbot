Air India allows up to 4 churns in Economy Class. After that limit is exceeded, the penalty is US$10 per additional churn (per passenger per segment). 

Note: An admin fee of 10.5% of the total ADM is added, and for ADMs issued in India GST @18% applies to both the ADM value and the admin fee. Air India may also take further action (e.g., inventory access restrictions) for repeated non‑compliant booking practices.
