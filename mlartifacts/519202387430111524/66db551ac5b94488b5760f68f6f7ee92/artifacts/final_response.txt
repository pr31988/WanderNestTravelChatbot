For Economy Class bookings Air India allows up to 4 churns. After that, the penalty is an ADM of US$10 for each additional churn per passenger per segment.

Note: An admin fee of 10.5% of the ADM value is added, and for ADMs issued in India GST @18% applies to both the ADM value and the admin fee (effective 01 Oct 2023). Air India may also apply other actions or amend charges at its discretion.
