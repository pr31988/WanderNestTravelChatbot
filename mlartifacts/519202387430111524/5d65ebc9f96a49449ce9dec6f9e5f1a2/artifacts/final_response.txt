Under the Fair Credit Billing Act you must send your dispute notice so the card issuer receives it no later than 60 days after the date you received the first monthly statement that listed the charge for the ticket.

Do this as soon as possible and send your dispute to the address on your monthly statement that is specifically for disputing charges (this may be different from the payment address). Include:
- your account number;
- a photocopy of the ticket, itinerary, or receipt;
- identification of any used and unused flight segments (if partially used);
- a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy, you did not receive the service, and you are requesting a credit pursuant to the Fair Credit Billing Act.

Keep copies of everything and consider sending by certified mail or another trackable method. If you also want to pursue the bankruptcy proceeding, you can file a proof of claim there, but recoveries in bankruptcy are often limited.
