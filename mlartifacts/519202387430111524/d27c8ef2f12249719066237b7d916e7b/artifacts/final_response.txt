According to the information from the U.S. Department of Transportation in our knowledge base, the following countries require disinsection of all inbound flights using an aerosolized spray while passengers are on board:

- Ecuador (only for Galápagos and inter-island flights)  
- Grenada  
- Guyana  
- India  
- Kiribati  
- Madagascar  
- Panama  
- Seychelles  
- Tanzania  
- Timor‑Leste  
- Trinidad and Tobago  
- Uruguay  
- Zimbabwe

Please note: policies can change. Travelers should confirm disinsection rules with their airline or travel agent before travel. The World Health Organization and ICAO allow alternative methods (e.g., residual treatment) in some countries, and the DOT notes disinsection, when done properly, is not expected to pose a health risk though some people may experience transient discomfort.
