Because the U.S.–foreign segment is 14 hours (i.e., over 12 hours), the DOT’s longer international standard applies: the airline must refund the checked-bag fee if it fails to deliver your bag within 30 hours of your flight arriving at the gate — provided you have filed a Mishandled Baggage Report with the carrier.

What to do next (practical steps)
- File a Mishandled Baggage Report with the airline as soon as the bag is found to be missing.  
- Keep any receipts and documentation.  
- If the bag isn’t delivered within 30 hours of arrival, request a refund of the baggage fee from the airline.
