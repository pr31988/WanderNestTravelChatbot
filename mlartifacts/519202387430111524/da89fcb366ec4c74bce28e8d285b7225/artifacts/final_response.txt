Air India allows up to 4 churns in Economy. After that, the penalty is an ADM of US$10 for each additional churn per passenger per segment.

Note: ADMs are charged per passenger per segment. In addition, an admin fee equal to 10.5% of the ADM amount plus applicable GST (18%) on both the ADM and the admin fee is applied for ADMs issued in India (effective 01 Oct 2023). Repeated non‑compliant behaviour may also lead to further actions (e.g., blocking access to Air India inventory).
