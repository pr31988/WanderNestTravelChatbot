According to the provided Travel Knowledge Base (U.S. DOT Aircraft Disinsection guidance), the following countries require disinsection of all inbound flights by an aerosolized spray while passengers are on board:

- Ecuador (only for Galápagos and inter‑island flights)  
- Grenada  
- Guyana  
- India  
- Kiribati  
- Madagascar  
- Panama  
- Seychelles  
- Tanzania  
- Timor‑Leste  
- Trinidad and Tobago  
- Uruguay  
- Zimbabwe

Note: this information comes from the U.S. Department of Transportation summary and policies can change. If you’re flying to any of these destinations, please verify current requirements with your airline, travel agent, or the destination’s official authorities before travel.
