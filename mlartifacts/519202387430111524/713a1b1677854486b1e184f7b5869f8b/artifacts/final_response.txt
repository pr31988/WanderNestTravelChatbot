Generally you must notify your credit card issuer within 60 days. Specifically, the Fair Credit Billing Act requires that the card issuer receive your dispute no later than 60 days after the date you received the first monthly statement that listed the airline charge.

Practical steps (from DOT guidance)
- Send your dispute in writing to the address for disputing charges shown on your monthly statement (this can be different from the payment address).  
- Include: your account number; a photocopy of the ticket/itinerary/receipt; if you used part of the travel, identify used and unused segments; and a statement that the airline is in bankruptcy, you did not receive the service, and you are requesting a credit under the Fair Credit Billing Act.  
- File as soon as possible — even if you believe you are within 60 days — and keep copies and proof of mailing.
