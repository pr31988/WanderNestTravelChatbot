According to the information provided (U.S. Dept. of Transportation summary), the following countries require disinsection of all inbound flights using an aerosolized spray while passengers are on board:

- Ecuador (only for flights to the Galápagos and inter‑island flights)  
- Grenada  
- Guyana  
- India  
- Kiribati  
- Madagascar  
- Panama  
- Seychelles  
- Tanzania  
- Timor‑Leste  
- Trinidad and Tobago  
- Uruguay  
- Zimbabwe

Note: policies can change. Travelers should confirm disinsection requirements with their airline, travel agent, or the destination country’s official authorities before travel (especially if you have health concerns about aerosol treatment).
